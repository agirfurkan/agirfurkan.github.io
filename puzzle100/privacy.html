English

Puzzle100 is a puzzle game made by Furkan Ağır. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Your game data stays on your device. Scores, statistics, settings and saved games are stored only on your iPhone. We do not run servers and we do not collect this data.

Game Center. If you sign in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center and handled under Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising. The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy choices. On iOS you can allow or deny tracking in the system prompt and later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Notifications are scheduled locally on your device; no data is sent anywhere.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Contact: agirfurkan@gmail.com
