Puzzle100

Fill the grid from 1 to 100.

How to play

Each number goes 3 cells straight (skipping 2) or 2 cells diagonally (skipping 1) from the one before. Keep going until the board is full or you run out of moves.

FAQ

How is the score calculated?
10 points for every number you reach. Hints cost 10 points, extra undos 5 and a rewind 30, so games played without help rank highest.

When does the daily puzzle change?
Every day at 00:00 UTC. Everyone gets the same board, and you have one attempt a day.

I bought “Remove ads” on another device.
Open Settings → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Settings → Privacy choices (EU/UK), or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
