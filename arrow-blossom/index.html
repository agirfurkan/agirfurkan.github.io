Arrow Blossom

A calm brain puzzle: tap the arrows, clear the board.

How to play

Tap an arrow. If its path is clear it slides off the board; if not, it bounces back. Clear every arrow to finish the level. Hold an arrow to preview its path.

FAQ

When does the daily race change?
Every day at 00:00 UTC. Everyone plays the same board and only your first try counts. The 60-second rush ranking resets every week.

Why don't I see my rank?
Rankings use Game Center. Sign in under iOS Settings → Game Center. You can play everything without signing in.

I tap arrows by accident.
Turn on Settings → Tap twice to move.

I bought “Remove ads” on another device.
Open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Settings → Privacy options (EU/UK), or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
