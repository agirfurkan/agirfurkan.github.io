Sono: Extract Audio from Video

Get the sound out of any video on your iPhone and save it as M4A or WAV. Trim it and boost quiet recordings. Everything happens on your device — no account, no uploads.

How to use

  1. Tap Select Video (Photos) or Choose from Files.
  2. Optionally trim with the handles, turn on Boost volume or pick WAV.
  3. Tap Extract Audio, then Share or Save to Files.

Questions

Which videos work? Videos from the Photos app and MOV, MP4 and M4V files. MKV and WebM are not supported yet.

"This video has no sound track" — the video was recorded or saved without sound.

Restoring Pro: Settings › Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancelling the weekly plan: iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Sono.
