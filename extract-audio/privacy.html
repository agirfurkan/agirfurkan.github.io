Sono — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Sono (Extract Audio from Video) is made by Furkan Ağır. This page explains what the app does with your data.

We don't collect data. The app has no account, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK, and there is no developer server.

Your videos and audio stay on your iPhone. The videos you choose are read on your device to take their sound. The extracted audio files are saved inside the app on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Nothing is uploaded.

Photos access. The app only sees the videos you pick in the system picker.

Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app stores, on your device, whether you have used the free extraction.

Sharing. When you share a file, it goes only where you send it, through the iOS share sheet.

Children. The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
